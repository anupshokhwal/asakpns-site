Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains how ASAKPNS ("we", "us", "our") handles personal data on our website (asakpns.com) and in the mobile and web applications we publish ("Apps"), in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act").

1. Who we are

ASAKPNS is a sole proprietorship based in Pune, Maharashtra, India. For privacy questions, contact support@asakpns.com.

2. Our website

Our website is for information only. It has no sign-up or contact forms, sets no tracking cookies and runs no analytics.

  • If you email us: we use your email address and message only to reply, and delete them when no longer needed.
  • Hosting: our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver the site and protect it from attacks.

3. Our apps

This section applies only to our Apps. An App may also have its own privacy notice with details specific to it; where it does, that notice applies together with this policy.

What an App may collect

  • Information you give us: such as your name, email address or phone number when you create an account or contact support.
  • Usage and device data: device model, operating system, app version and crash reports, to keep the App working.
  • Data for specific features: such as location, camera or files, only when a feature needs it and only with your permission, which you can withdraw in your device settings.

Each App's store listing shows exactly what it collects. We do not sell your personal data.

How we use it

To provide and improve the App, give support, keep it secure and prevent misuse, and meet legal obligations.

Service providers

We use trusted providers such as Google Cloud and Firebase for hosting, analytics and crash reporting. They process data on our instructions and only for the purposes above.

Where data is stored

Data may be stored on servers in India or other countries operated by our service providers. For government and public-sector projects, data is hosted in India as the contract and applicable guidelines require.

How long we keep it

Only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, after which we delete or anonymise it. Apps with accounts explain how to delete your account and data, in the App or by request.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect personal data, including encryption in transit, access controls and two-step verification for staff accounts. If a breach affects you, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

Children

Our Apps are not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect their personal data without verifiable parental consent as the law requires.

4. Your rights

Whether you emailed us or use an App, under the DPDP Act you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw consent, and nominate someone to exercise your rights. If you are outside India, you also keep the rights your own country's data protection laws give you, such as the GDPR in Europe. Email support@asakpns.com.

5. Grievance officer

For complaints about how we handle personal data:

Email: support@asakpns.com
We aim to respond within 30 days.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page, with the date it was last updated.
